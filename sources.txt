# Suttara — Source Materials and Credits

Mahāsatipaṭṭhāna Sutta (DN 22) — The Great Discourse on the Establishing of Mindfulness

A reference list of Pāli texts, English translations, spoken recordings and chanting for use in developing Suttara.

## 1. Written texts

Pāli and English — Interlinear Edition

- Editor and translator: Ānandajoti Bhikkhu
- First published: November 2005
- Third revised edition: October 2011
- Description: Pāli and English presented line by line, with annotations, doctrinal explanations and section divisions.
- Source: [Ancient Buddhist Texts — Interlinear Edition](https://ancient-buddhist-texts.net/Texts-and-Translations/Satipatthana/index.htm?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Pāli — Established Text

- Editor: Ānandajoti Bhikkhu
- Edition: October 2011
- Description: Pāli text with grammatical notes, variant readings and an explanation of how the edition was established.
- Source: [Ancient Buddhist Texts — Pāli Edition](https://ancient-buddhist-texts.net/Buddhist-Texts/D22-Mahasatipatthana/index.htm?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

English — The Long Discourse about the Ways of Attending to Mindfulness

- Translator: Ānandajoti Bhikkhu
- Edition: October 2011
- Description: Complete English translation, with explanatory material and an accompanying spoken reading.
- Source: [Ancient Buddhist Texts — English Edition](https://ancient-buddhist-texts.net/English-Texts/Mindfulness/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

All three editions are published by Ancient Buddhist Texts. Ānandajoti's text was established through comparison of several traditional editions, including the Buddha Jayanti Tripiṭaka, Burmese, and Pali Text Society editions.&#x20;

## 2. Spoken recordings

Pāli — Ordinary spoken recitation

- Reader: Ānandajoti Bhikkhu
- Language: Pāli
- Style: Plain spoken reading, rather than melodic chanting.
- Description: Suitable for studying pronunciation, rhythm and individual passages.
- Source: [Ancient Buddhist Texts — Audio Library](https://ancient-buddhist-texts.net/Audio/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

English — Spoken translation

- Translation: Ānandajoti Bhikkhu
- Language: English
- Description: Spoken reading accompanying the English translation.
- Source: [English Edition with Audio](https://ancient-buddhist-texts.net/English-Texts/Mindfulness/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The two readings are particularly useful for Suttara's language-learning features because they accompany the written material.&#x20;

## Credits and acknowledgements

The Mahāsatipaṭṭhāna Sutta is part of the Dīgha Nikāya (DN 22) within the Pāli Buddhist canon.

Primary textual scholarship: Ānandajoti Bhikkhu, for editing, translating, annotating and publishing the editions provided by Ancient Buddhist Texts.

Spoken Pāli and English: Ānandajoti Bhikkhu, for the accompanying readings and educational recordings.

Traditional chanting: S. N. Goenka, for the Pāli chanting recordings associated with the Vipassana tradition of Sayagyi U Ba Khin.

Publishing and preservation: Ancient Buddhist Texts, Pariyatti and the Vipassana Research Institute, for making textual and recorded teaching materials accessible.

Usage note: These credits identify the contributors and source publishers; they do not establish permission to redistribute every recording or translation. Suttara should retain source attribution and record licensing information separately for each downloaded asset.
